Viewed 2026-10-12 (Asia/Seoul). References inform furniture composition only; they do not replace ROOMFIT interface tokens or claim exact reproduction of products/photos.

- [IKEA — How to create a Scandi-style living room](https://www.ikea.com/gb/en/rooms/living-room/how-to/how-to-create-a-scandi-style-living-room-pxm3PLt85sQrcSelXTnO3u61Q/): natural/light timber alongside light neutrals; restrained colour accents; closed storage can use lighter finishes to soften visual mass.
- [IKEA — Create a calm oasis in your living room](https://www.ikea.com/ca/en/rooms/living-room/gallery/calm-living-room-oasis-puba0e66ba0/): coordinated subdued shades and accent colours instead of a completely monochrome room.

Applied interpretation: keep original ROOMFIT multi-role palette values; distribute primary/secondary shades across textile and painted surfaces; balance with warm neutral mattresses/carcasses and light/dark timber; preserve optical/biological materials and metallic fittings. Role assignment is our implementation judgment, not a colour formula supplied by IKEA. Screen colour/material appearance is approximate and does not imply real SKU colour availability.

## Automatic combinations (2026-10-12)

- User-supplied colour palette screenshot: teal/mint, blush/blue/navy, mint/yellow/peach, warm neutrals with powder blue, and contrasting dark/accent families. Curated furniture palettes use subdued finish variants; they do not assert exact sampled hex values from the screenshot.

Applied interpretation: six stable named combinations ranked by style/lifestyle/floor, with large furniture restrained and textiles/accessories carrying supporting/accent colours. Original wood/PBR/protected materials stay distinct. Colour roles approximate composition; furniture counts and source part assignments cannot guarantee exact surface-area percentages.
